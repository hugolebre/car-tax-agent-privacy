# Privacy Policy — Car Tax Agent

**Last updated: 9 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information the **Car Tax Agent** mobile app
("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By
using the app you agree to this policy.

Car Tax Agent helps you estimate the one-time registration tax and the annual
road tax for a used or imported car in several European countries.

## Summary

- We only collect what the app needs to work: your **account details** (if you
  create one) and the **cars you choose to save**.
- Reading a car listing with the **camera** or the **on-screen overlay** is done
  **entirely on your device**. Those images and screen contents are **never
  uploaded to us or stored**.
- The free version shows ads through **Google AdMob**.
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

## Information we collect

**Account information (only if you sign in).**
If you create an account or continue as a guest, we store an account identifier
and, for email accounts, your **email address**. Authentication is handled by our
backend provider, **Supabase**. Passwords are managed by Supabase and are not
visible to us.

**Your saved cars ("Garage").**
When you save a car, the details you entered — such as engine size, CO₂, fuel
type, registration date, power, price, the country you selected, and a label you
give it — are stored so you can see and compare them later. If you are signed in,
they are stored in your account (via Supabase) so they sync across your devices.
If you use the app without an account, your garage is stored on your device and
tied to an anonymous account. **Row-Level Security** ensures each account can only
access its own saved cars.

**Feedback (only if you send it).**
If you use "Help us improve", we collect your rating and message, an optional
email address, and basic context (app version, the country selected in the app,
device model, and Android version). This is submitted to a private Google Form
that we own.

**Advertising data.**
The free version displays ads via **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and process
device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in
Google's policies.

**Processed on your device, never collected.**
To read a car listing, the app uses your **camera** or a one-time, user-consented
**screen capture** and runs **on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit)**. The
captured images and screen contents are processed locally and are **not** sent to
us or to any server, and are **not** stored by the app.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or precise identifiers, and the app
is not directed to children.

## How we use information

- To provide the core features: calculating taxes and saving/syncing your garage.
- To let you sign in and keep your garage across devices.
- To understand and fix problems and improve the app (feedback).
- To display ads in the free version.

## Who we share information with (service providers)

We use the following processors, each under their own privacy terms:

- **Supabase** — account authentication and database for your saved cars.
  https://supabase.com/privacy
- **Google AdMob** — advertising in the free version.
  https://policies.google.com/privacy · https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- **Google Forms** — receives feedback you choose to send.
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google ML Kit** — on-device text recognition (no data leaves the device).
  https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

We do not sell your personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural
advertising beyond the ad services described above.

## Data retention and your choices

- **Your saved cars** stay until you delete them. You can remove any car in the
  app, and you can sign out at any time.
- **Delete your account / data:** email us (below) and we will delete your account
  and its saved cars from Supabase.
- **Ads / feedback data** are retained by Google according to their policies.

## Permissions we request

- **Display over other apps** and **screen capture (media projection)** — to show
  the optional floating button and read the listing you are viewing. Screen capture
  is user-initiated and processed on-device.
- **Camera** — to scan a listing. Processed on-device.
- **Internet / network state** — to sync your garage, load ads, and send feedback.
- **Notifications** — to show the ongoing notification while the floating button
  is active.

## Security

Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Access to your saved cars is
restricted to your own account by Supabase Row-Level Security.

## International transfers

Our providers (Supabase, Google) may process data on servers located in the EU or
other countries, subject to their own safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top
shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept
the updated policy.

# Delete your Car Tax Agent account and data

You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time. You do
not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

## Delete individual cars (in the app)

Open **My Garage** in the app and tap the trash icon on any saved car to remove
it. It is deleted from your account straight away.

## Delete your entire account and all data

Email **monitoring@hyteps.nl** with the subject **"Delete my account"**, from the
email address on your account (or include that email in the message). We will
permanently delete your account and all of your saved cars from our systems
(Supabase) within **30 days**.

- **Deleted:** your account record (email / account identifier) and every car you
  saved (label, specifications, and the country you selected).
- **Retained:** nothing that identifies you. Any aggregated or anonymised logs
  cannot be linked back to you.

## Guest (used without creating an account)

If you used the app as a guest, your saved cars are tied to an anonymous account.
You can delete them in **My Garage**, or uninstall the app. To have anonymous data
removed from our backend as well, email us as above and mention that you used a
guest account.

## Ads and feedback

Advertising is provided by Google; see the [Privacy Policy](./) and Google's
policies. Feedback you sent through the in-app form can also be deleted on request
to the same email address.
